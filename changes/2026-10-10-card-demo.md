title: A guided simulation in the first KK gluon card
---

what

The First KK gluon card has a 🎬 Demo button. In about forty seconds it loads the published
warped reference point, reads r against the ATLAS tt̄ limit, shows the m(tt̄) spectrum with its
interference, switches to the flat GHU coloron, and sets the SM theory uncertainty to zero, with a
banner explaining each step and a closing panel on how to read the card. A link with
`demo=kkgluon` starts it; `lang=es` gives it in Spanish.

why

A card with nine controls teaches little until someone moves one. The demonstration presses the
real buttons, so a reader can see the tool answer before choosing their own point.

so

Nothing in the demonstration is precomputed: every number in the banner is read from the model at
that step. The banner can be closed at any time, and the card is left editable in the state the
demonstration reached.
