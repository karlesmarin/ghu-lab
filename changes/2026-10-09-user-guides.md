what

Forty-two guides in English and Spanish explain every menu section, Simulator mode and embedded
experiment, plus neutrino decays and getting started. Search by task, browse by family, follow
an example and inspect the assumptions. The app opens the relevant guide without resetting the
calculation; language changes retain the page. Both manuals are generated from the same records,
with Babel composing the Spanish PDF.

why

Help was distributed across short panels and technical notes. The maintained catalogues now
supply inline help, a 59-term glossary, full HTML guides and manuals. Older glossary wording has
been scoped to its gauge seed, moment approximation, boundary conditions and comparison hypothesis.
These clarifications do not change numerical formulas or frozen paper evidence.

so

Home, Docs, Editions, both repository READMEs and the machine-readable inventory link the guides.
Current pages have canonical URLs; English and Spanish guides declare reciprocal alternatives.
The root sitemap includes all current guide pages, with no fragment-only model states or frozen
archives presented as separate current tools. Coverage, routes, formula parity, links, browser
behaviour and PDF typesetting are checked before publication. Discovery aids do not guarantee
search-engine indexing.
