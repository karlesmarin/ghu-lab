The two charts in chapter 38 contain the actual maintained observed limits: ten ATLAS points and seventy CMS points, with separate hypotheses, units and logarithmic vertical ranges. Data can support a physical realization meeting a theorem's hypotheses. They cannot replace a proof or show that an arithmetic example describes nature without an observable prediction. ATLAS/CMS limits are not measured new-particle production rates; staying below a limit is not a discovery. CERN hosts the LHC and is not a third independent dataset.

The figure data and source hashes are included in the film's `inputs.json`. Cross-section densities, published resonance upper limits and theoretical potentials are different quantities and use different panels. There is no compatible GHU-to-top-pair prediction or observed fit in this card.

The ten new Lean statements formalize rational algebra. Exact matrix positivity applies to published decimal strings. Three pointwise logarithm-derivative bounds do not certify the full Bessel integral. The approximately 7.4% quartic fit/table discrepancy remains open.
