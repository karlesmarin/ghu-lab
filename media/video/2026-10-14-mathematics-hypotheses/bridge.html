Assumptions → certificate → module → observable

A proof certifies an implication. Physics determines whether the actual fields, backgrounds and action satisfy its hypotheses. An experimental comparison additionally needs a prediction with uncertainties.

Measured data and the figures

The ATLAS curve contains ten published observed 95% CL limits on σ×BR(tt̄), for the RS KK-gluon benchmark with Γ/M=30%, at 13 TeV and 140 fb⁻¹. CMS contains seventy observed narrow qq-resonance limits on σ×B×A, at 13 TeV and 137 fb⁻¹. The vertical axes are logarithmic and use different ranges. Points are the maintained source values; straight segments only guide the eye. They are not measured new-particle production rates. CERN is the laboratory hosting the LHC, not a third independent dataset.

Neither search reports a significant new-particle signal. Experimental evidence can strengthen the applicability of a physical model satisfying a theorem’s assumptions; it cannot turn a conditional theorem into an unconditional physical claim.
