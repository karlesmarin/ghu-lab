title: The first KK gluon against ATLAS, CMS dijet and CMS m(tt̄) data
---

what

A ninth experiment card, Collider → First KK gluon at the LHC. Choose the flat GHU coloron or a
warped KK gluon with zero-mode quarks of bulk mass c; read couplings, widths and branching
fractions; compare LO σ × BR(tt̄) with the ATLAS observed limit and σ × B × A(dijet) with the CMS
spin-1 limits interpolated to the width, as r = prediction / limit. The card also computes the
m(tt̄) spectrum with the interference between the KK gluon and the QCD gluon in the bins of the
CMS parton-level measurement, and the expected Δχ² against its covariance.

why

The collider section compared a mass with a bound; an experimental limit is on σ × BR, so the
meaningful comparison is a rate. A broad KK gluon also changes the tt̄ spectrum below its pole
through interference, whose sign depends on the couplings: destructive for flat GHU, constructive
for the published warped reference point.

so

Every number carries its status and witness. Couplings are checked against a 40-digit reference;
ATLAS's and CMS's own theory curves are reproduced; the spectrum agrees with an independent
computation from Dirac traces and LHAPDF, recomputed in the page. A crossing is a comparison with
each experiment's benchmark and the spectrum Δχ² is a sensitivity, not an exclusion. LO only;
four-top production and fermion KK modes are not computed.
