what
: A chaptered Full HD video guide demonstrates all 29 menu sections, three Simulator modes, eight research cards and integrated diagnostics. English and Spanish versions include synthetic narration, timed captions, searchable chapters, transcripts and downloads.

why
: A description of a control is easier to follow when the control, its changed value and the resulting calculation are visible together. The video keeps the scientific assumptions beside the outputs.

so
: The home page, documentation, navigation and both repository READMEs link to the guide. The instrument and its scientific calculations are unchanged. Only the video page loads adjacent, versioned media; it does not depend on an external video service.
