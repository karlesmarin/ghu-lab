what

The tenth embedded research card adds four reusable calculations: a flat Robin boundary
spectrum, complex profile Gram rank, exact parity-inserted half-spin traces, and a restricted
six-dimensional SU(2) tensor condition. The map, rotatable-perspective surface and spectrum
slice share the same computed masses. Selecting points updates the physical inputs; the
camera angle changes only the view. SVG and JSON exports retain the grid and input values.

English/Español switches the new card's controls, results, graph labels, messages and demo
without resetting inputs. The Spanish guide uses the translated control names; the general
video has separate English and Spanish captures so the new Spanish chapter shows Spanish results.

why

An unwanted zero mode, linearly dependent profiles and an anomaly obstruction are different
failure mechanisms. A diagnostic should expose which requirement fails without turning a
partial success into a complete model verdict. Nonzero overlaps do not establish dependence,
and factorized anomaly polynomials do not establish an integral tensor charge lattice.

so

The worked examples, assumptions, formulas, API, failure cases and independent reference
calculations are documented in docs/boundary-research.md. The 44 English/Spanish guides and
their manuals include the new card. The general video revision 2026-10-12-boundary-research
has 45 chapters and 154 scenes in each language. The 10 October Demo revision keeps its
original page and media. Tool version is 1.0.0; the application release is 0.2.1.

Numerical references use SciPy, NumPy and Fraction; browser checks exercise graph selection,
numeric controls, saved links, exports and mobile layout. The full local/global anomaly
calculation, normalized coefficients, integral charge lattice, Wilson vacuum, realistic flavour
and neutrino masses remain open. None of the frozen paper records is revised by this addition.
