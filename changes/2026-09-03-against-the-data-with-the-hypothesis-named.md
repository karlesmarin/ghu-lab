what
: The vacuum fixes m_W·R, a number with no units. One measured mass makes the whole ladder
  dimensionful: 1/R = m_W / (m_W·R), and each field's first massive state follows in GeV.
  `src/kernel/experiment.mjs` holds the measured numbers the instrument uses, each with its
  source, its URL and the date it was read — m_W (PDG 2025, the LHC-TeV working-group average,
  not the CDF 2022 value), m_h and m_t (PDG 2024), the CMS full-Run-2 dijet limits (axigluons and
  colorons 6.6 TeV, excited quarks 6.3, Z′ 2.9), ATLAS's dilepton Z′_ψ at 4.5 TeV, the CMS
  dijet-angular contact scales of 17 and 37 TeV (CMS-EXO-24-011, the analysis Part VIII
  transports to), and Super-Kamiokande's τ/B(p → e⁺π⁰) > 2.4 × 10³⁴ years. The harness refuses
  an entry without a source or a date.

  `vac5Confront` applies exactly one bound, the one that needs no Standard-Model embedding to be
  applied: the first Kaluza–Klein level of the unbroken vectors sits at 1/R, and if colour lives
  in the bulk that level is a colour-octet vector with coloron-like couplings. The verdict says
  *above* or *below the bound* and names the hypothesis in the sentence.

why
: A number a model builder can act on is a mass in GeV against a limit from a detector, not a
  Wilson-line angle. And the W that sets the unit has to be a Wilson-line mass: at a symmetric
  vacuum the lightest massive vector is the first KK mode at 1/R, which the Wilson line had
  nothing to do with, and calling it the W would fix the scale from nothing. So the unit is the
  lightest vector strictly inside the tower, and a symmetric vacuum reports that nothing sets its
  scale rather than a number.

so
: What is deliberately not done: which massless piece is a quark, the hypercharges, sin²θ_W,
  the Higgs mass from V″, the proton-decay rate. Each needs the Standard-Model cell at the
  vacuum or a normalisation anchored to a published number, and each is listed in the HANDOFF
  as the next tool rather than approximated here. A bound applied to a state it was not set for
  is a false verdict, and the instrument would rather print one line it can defend.
